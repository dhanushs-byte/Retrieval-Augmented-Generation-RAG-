Company Leave Policy (fictional training document)
Employees receive 12 casual leaves per calendar year and 10 sick leaves per calendar year.
Planned leave should be requested at least 3 working days in advance through the HR portal.
The employee's reporting manager approves or rejects each leave request.
Unused casual leave cannot be carried forward to the next calendar year.
For emergency leave, the employee should inform the reporting manager as soon as possible and submit the request in the HR portal when able.
